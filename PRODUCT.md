# Find the Cats

## Platform

web

## Stack

React, TypeScript, and SVG, as requested in the supplied brief.

## Product purpose

Explore pencil illustrations of Paris, Venice, Kyoto, Lisbon, Istanbul, Amsterdam, Prague, Chefchaouen, Havana, and Seoul and find six cats in each picture.
The picture leads immediately, with only a small cat icon and counter over its top-right corner.
Clicking a cat lightens it and updates a small counter.
Finding every cat automatically loads another picture.

## Constraints

- Keep the playable page simple and usable on phones.
- Support zooming and dragging without accidental selections.
- Keep progress and a shuffled scene queue in browser storage.
- Provide a development-only polygon editor for maintaining artwork targets.
- Follow the user's git workflow; publish a pull request only when requested.

## Visual commitments

Detailed graphite illustrations on textured paper, portrait composition, with cats integrated into the architecture.
Fit the complete illustration into the available viewport without cropping cats or distorting the artwork.
Keep the counter unboxed, with no visible location heading, footer instructions, or zoom controls.
Adapt the counter's ink to light and dark artwork, including while zooming and panning.
No landing page, navigation, account flow, or surrounding dashboard.
